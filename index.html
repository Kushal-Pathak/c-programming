How to study

  1. Read each lesson and understand its definitions and examples.
  2. Try the interactive demonstrations and practice questions.
  3. Complete the scored quizzes and review your mistakes.
  4. Mark lessons as studied and attempt the final unit review.

Lesson progress and quiz scores are saved locally in your browser when storage is available. Learning materials support examination preparation; actual marks depend on understanding and practice.
